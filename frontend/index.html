What the forecast shows and how to read the 0–100 index

MIFE combines regional temperature and rainfall with spotting bug biology. It provides one combined forecast for fruitspotting bug (FSB, Amblypelta nitida) and banana spotting bug (BSB, A. lutescens lutescens); separate species curves are not produced. Most field validation evidence is for FSB, so BSB-specific performance remains less certain.

The index is relative and has no units: 100 is the highest modelled nymph-plus-adult activity within each scenario during the displayed season. Low is 0–<10, moderate 10–<30, high 30–<70 and very high 70–100. These are activity bands—not economic, damage or treatment thresholds. See the grower manual for model details.
